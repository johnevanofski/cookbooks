Weigh the coffee on a scale before grinding. Water in milliliters (mL). Water ≈ 200–205°F (just off the boil).

Brew Steps

  1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water; discard the rinse water.
  2. Add ground coffee, level the bed.
  3. Bloom: pour ~2× the coffee weight in water (e.g. 30 mL for 15 g coffee). Wait 30–45 sec.
  4. Pour the rest slowly in circles, in 2–3 pours, keeping the bed covered.
  5. Total time: 2:30–3:30 for 1–2 cups, 3:30–4:30 for 4 cups.

Grind Size

Medium-fineLike table salt. Best for cone drippers (V60, Chemex needs a bit coarser).
MediumLike sand. Good for flat-bottom drippers (Kalita, Melitta).
Medium-coarseLike coarse sand. Chemex or 4-cup batches.

Sour/thin? Grind finer. Bitter/harsh? Grind coarser. Adjust one step at a time.

Coffee Types

Light roastBright, fruity. Slightly finer grind, hotter water (205°F).
Medium roastBalanced, sweet. Default settings above.
Dark roastBold, low acidity. Slightly coarser grind, cooler water (195–200°F).
Single originDistinct flavors; great for pour over, medium to light roast.
BlendsConsistent and forgiving; good everyday choice.

Use fresh beans (roasted within ~4 weeks) and grind right before brewing.
