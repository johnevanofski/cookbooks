🍚 Cooking Rice

Stovetop, covered pot. Rice in cups, water in milliliters (1 cup = 240 mL).

Basic Method

  1. Rinse the rice in a bowl or strainer until the water runs mostly clear (skip for risotto rice).
  2. Add rice, water and a pinch of salt to a pot with a tight lid.
  3. Bring to a boil, then cover and drop to the lowest heat.
  4. Simmer for the time listed. Don't lift the lid.
  5. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for the time listed.
  6. Fluff with a fork (or fold gently with a paddle for sushi rice).

Ratios work with any scoop, so 1 part rice to the listed parts water. Amounts are a starting point; pots and heat vary, so adjust by about 2 tablespoons (30 mL) of water per cup of rice if it comes out too firm or too soft.
